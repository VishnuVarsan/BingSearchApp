About TechTips Hub

Our Mission

TechTips Hub is an independent technology resource dedicated to providing clear, accurate, and actionable guides for home users, students, and small business teams. We believe technology should be accessible to everyone — not just IT professionals.

Every guide we publish is written by a subject-matter expert, tested on real hardware or software, and updated whenever underlying products change. We do not accept paid placements, sponsored rankings, or affiliate-only content without original added value.

Editorial Standards

Our content adheres to the following principles:

  • Original authorship: All guides are written and tested in-house by our technology team.
  • Accuracy: Facts, steps, and settings are verified against official documentation before publication.
  • Freshness: Guides are reviewed and revised whenever software updates or hardware changes affect the instructions.
  • Independence: Editorial decisions are not influenced by advertisers or product vendors.
  • Accessibility: Pages are structured with semantic HTML, proper heading hierarchy, and descriptive alt text to serve all users.

Our Team

TechTips Hub was founded in 2022 by a group of technology professionals with backgrounds in systems engineering, cybersecurity, and technical writing. The team collectively holds over 40 years of hands-on experience across Windows, macOS, Linux, mobile platforms, and enterprise networking.

Get in Touch

Have a question, a guide suggestion, or found an error? Contact us — we read every message and respond to most within two business days.
